## Review boundaries

- Several government pages returned access errors, timeouts or JavaScript-only
  shells when opened directly. Their indexed official-page text was used only
  for the visible, bounded claims. Live authenticated workflows were not tested.
- NID correction uses the current services FAQ, not the older NIDW correction
  page with historical distribution instructions. Document alternatives retain
  their applicant-category conditions.
- Passport enrolment uses notice 34 and the general instructions. Older
  checklists are not treated as a universal age/identity rule.
- The NBR FAQ contains historical assessment-year instructions. Only general
  navigation and record preparation are summarized; historical upload waivers,
  amendment deadlines and filing obligations are not applied to the current year.
- BRTA pages contain old fee tables and differing payment descriptions. No
  historical amount or guaranteed delivery interval was added. The official
  portal/calculator remains the source for a current transaction.
- Online GD describes complaint processing, not a guarantee of acceptance or
  immediate emergency response. The emergency record points separately to
  Bangladesh Police's national emergency contact.
- The catalogue intentionally leaves unsupported topics without exact records.
  The coverage audit reports those gaps rather than duplicating generic text
  under all taxonomy IDs.

## Maintenance

Reopen each source before a public release and at least every 90 days. Recheck
more frequently when an authority announces a change. Amend affected guidance,
retain important applicability conditions in displayed fields, update its review
date only after review, and rerun the corpus audit and tests. Remove an exact
record if its claims can no longer be supported, allowing labelled fallback.
